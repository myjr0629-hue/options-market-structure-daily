Earnings week Oct 26-30, 2026: how big a move do options price in?

Priced at the 2026-10-23 close · 50 US companies reporting this week · compiled by SIGNUM HQ

What the number is

  • Implied move = (call mid + put mid at the strike nearest the price) ÷ stock price, for the nearest listed expiry, using closing mids of the 2026-10-23 session.
  • It is the size the options market is pricing, not a direction and not a forecast. The realized move can land inside or outside it.
  • The straddle also covers the days between the report and expiry. Weekly expiries (Oct 30) sit right after the report; monthly expiries (Nov 20) include extra weeks, so those figures read larger.
